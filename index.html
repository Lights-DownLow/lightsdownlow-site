About

Lights Down Low started in 2006 in a tiny underground bar in San Francisco. Twenty years on it runs out of warehouses in Downtown Los Angeles, with stops in Tokyo and wherever else would have us.

Corey Sizemore and Richie Panic started it and are still the residents. The aim has not changed: keep it stimulating, somewhat satisfying and always sonically interesting.

House, techno, post-punk, disco, EBM, electro, industrial. Sometimes all in one night.
